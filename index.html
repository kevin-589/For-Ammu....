How I Found You ❤️

One train ride changed everything.

22 / 07 🚆

The First Glance

I didn't have my bike that day, so I took the train from Tirupur to Coimbatore.

It was actually my first time travelling that way.

And then... I saw you.

I don't even know how long I was staring at you. 😂❤️

After some time, I got a place to sit near you.

And I didn't hesitate.

I sat beside you and started talking.

📱

The Instagram Conversation

I asked for your Instagram...

And you hesitated to give it to me. 😂

But when I was sitting beside you, I had already seen it.

When I reached college, I texted you.

And for the next two days, we kept talking continuously on Instagram.

Somehow, talking to you started becoming my favorite part of the day.

🏍️

The First Ride

Then one evening, I picked you up from the bus stand and dropped you home.

You were already worried about the police. 😂

And you were scared of speed too, so I became the most careful rider in the world that day. 😌

Then you told me you were hungry.

So we stopped at a tea shop and had some juice together.

It wasn't some fancy date. Just you, me, a little juice, and a memory I'll always keep.

🚌

I Came For You

The next day, I went to Coimbatore again.

But you didn't know why.

I came because I wanted to travel with you.

We sat together and came back to Tirupur.

Maybe you thought it was just another bus ride. I didn't.

☀️

A Little More Time

It was Sunday, and you had plans to visit your friend.

I picked you up and dropped you back, just so I could spend a little more time with you.

Maybe I was just finding excuses to be around you. 😌

That day, you also found out that I smoked.

You didn't like it.

And seeing that you cared enough to tell me made me think about it.

So I started reducing it. Because you matter to me.

27 / 07 🚆

The Goa Train

Then came 27 July.

Your office sent you to Goa for work.

3 PM. Railway station.

You were looking around for something...

And then you saw me standing beside you in your coach.

And I think that surprise on your face is something I'll remember for a long time.

We didn't talk much that day.

Then your train started moving.

You went to Goa.

And I stayed behind.

And now I'm missing you badly. ❤️

I'd still choose the seat beside you.

Every single time. ❤️
